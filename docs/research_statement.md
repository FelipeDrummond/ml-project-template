# Research statement

> Replace the stub fields below for your project. This is the source of truth
> for **why** the project exists. Update when scope shifts.

## Problem

What problem is being solved? What is the input and the desired output? Who
cares?

## Motivation

Why now? What gap in existing work or what concrete need motivates this?

## Hypothesis

The specific, falsifiable claim this project tests. Prefer "X improves Y by
at least Z under condition C" over "X is better than Y."

## Success criteria

Concrete metrics + thresholds that decide whether the hypothesis held.
Decide these *before* running experiments to avoid post-hoc moving goalposts.

- Metric 1: <name> ≥ <threshold> on <dataset/setting>
